Before you start

How do you experience teaching in English?

This self-assessment helps you reflect on your confidence and on how easy or difficult you find different teaching tasks in English. It takes about ten minutes.

There are no right or wrong answers, and this is not a language test. It shows how you perceive your own teaching in English today. Many experienced and highly proficient lecturers find some tasks harder than others, and that is exactly what makes the result useful.

Your answers stay with you. Nothing is sent or stored anywhere else. Your answers are kept only in this browser until you clear them, and you decide what to save as a PDF and what to share.
